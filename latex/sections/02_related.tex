\subsection{Artifact- and Frequency-Based Detection}
\label{sec:rw-artifact}

CNN-based detectors remain a practical baseline for still-image real/fake classification.
Early work showed that CNN-generated images leave transferable forensic cues that a ResNet-style classifier can exploit across GAN families~\cite{wang2020cnn}.
Subsequent studies emphasized local texture, upsampling footprints, and neighboring-pixel dependencies as more stable signals than global semantics alone.
Neighboring Pixel Relationships (NPR) treat local interdependence induced by generator upsampling as a transferable artifact cue and report strong cross-generator behavior under their official protocol~\cite{tan2024npr}.
We treat NPR as an \emph{external pretrained reference} rather than a training-matched competitor.

Frequency-domain cues---spectral peaks, mid/high-band energy, and residual spectra---have long been used to expose generative artifacts~\cite{frank2020freq,durall2020watch,corvi2023diffusion}.
Whether a frequency module helps is backbone- and protocol-dependent.
We instantiate LiteFreqNet~v2 (gated FFT-magnitude fusion) under the same from-scratch recipe as the CNN/SSM panels to test transferability of frequency injection, not to claim a universal spectral solution.

\subsection{Pretrained, Reconstruction-Based, and Modern Generalization Detectors}
\label{sec:rw-general}

A complementary line of work avoids learning a heavily tuned ``fake'' detector and instead probes large pretrained feature spaces.
UniversalFakeDetect (UnivFD) uses frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 features with a linear probe and demonstrates improved transfer to unseen generative families relative to end-to-end detectors trained on a single generator breed~\cite{ojha2023ufd}.
FatFormer adapts CLIP with forgery-aware image/frequency adapters and language-guided alignment~\cite{liu2024fatformer}.
VIB-Net filters task-irrelevant multimodal features via a variational information bottleneck~\cite{zhang2025vib}, while CO-SPY adaptively fuses semantic and pixel-level artifact cues and releases a large multi-generator evaluation suite~\cite{cheng2025cospy}.
Beyond Generation reframes detection as one-class modeling of low-level photographic features extracted with a diffusion model used as a denoiser rather than a generator~\cite{zhong2025beyond}.
B-Free emphasizes bias-controlled training pairs and content-aligned fakes to reduce spurious correlations that inflate apparent transfer~\cite{guillaro2025bfree}.
We cite these works as complementary protocol lessons rather than as training-matched baselines.
Diffusion-oriented detectors often exploit reconstruction error or diffusion-specific representations.
DIRE distinguishes real and generated images via the discrepancy between an input and its diffusion reconstruction~\cite{wang2023dire}.
AEROBLADE instead uses latent-diffusion autoencoder reconstruction error without detector training~\cite{ricker2024aeroblade}.
Such pipelines can improve robustness on diffusion imagery, but they introduce a heavy generative-model reconstruction stage that differs from the compact direct-classification setting studied here.
We therefore discuss DIRE, AEROBLADE, and related reconstruction approaches as literature context and do \emph{not} re-run them as efficiency-matched baselines.

Large-scale benchmarks further shape evaluation expectations.
GenImage provides a million-scale multi-generator suite with cross-generator and degraded-image protocols~\cite{zhu2023genimage}; many 2024--2025 detectors report GenImage ACC/AP under large training budgets.
Our study instead freezes a low-data DiffusionForensics training split and uses UniversalFakeDetect per-generator AUCs as the primary cross-generator summary, with GenImage treated as an important external-validity reference rather than a shared leaderboard (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}).

\subsection{Lightweight and Efficient AI-Generated Content (AIGC) Detection}
\label{sec:rw-lite}

Compact ImageNet-style backbones (EfficientNet, MobileNet, ShuffleNet) are widely used when parameter count and throughput matter~\cite{tan2019efficientnet,howard2019mobilenetv3,ma2018shufflenet}.
In this study they form the controlled compact CNN panel trained from scratch under a locked recipe.
MDPI venues have likewise published compact CNN/ViT detectors for synthetic-image recognition, typically on CIFAKE-style single-domain settings or spatial--frequency--wavelet fusion Transformers~\cite{bird2024cifake,lokner2024lightweight,cinar2025pvism,man2026mdp}.

Two recent lines are particularly relevant to the compact-detection setting.
\textbf{LAID} benchmarks off-the-shelf lightweight CNNs/ViTs on GenImage subsets across spatial, spectral, and fusion domains, emphasizing Params/FLOPs and adversarial robustness~\cite{chivaran2025laid}.
\textbf{LOTA} extracts millisecond-scale bit-plane noise patches for GenImage cross-generator ACC/AP and compares against reconstruction-heavy baselines~\cite{wang2025lota}.
LAID and LOTA address complementary settings with different data, pretraining, and evaluation metrics; Table~\ref{tab:positioning} summarizes these distinctions.

\subsection{State-Space Vision Models and Study Positioning}
\label{sec:rw-ssm}

Selective state-space models provide linear-time sequence modeling~\cite{gu2023mamba}, and Vision Mamba (Vim) shows that bidirectional SSM blocks can serve as efficient vision backbones~\cite{zhu2024vim}; related visual state-space backbones such as VMamba further explore this design space~\cite{liu2024vmamba}.
Our \textbf{LiteSSM-A} and \textbf{LiteSSM-B} classifiers are \emph{study-specific pure-PyTorch} detectors inspired by these ideas (LiteSSM-B uses a bidirectional SelectiveSSM design), not official reproductions of Vim, VMamba, or any vendor Mamba release.

Table~\ref{tab:protocol} situates representative external paradigms against our controlled setting.
Prior work has demonstrated strong cross-generator detection using large pretrained feature extractors, reconstruction-based diffusion representations, generator-artifact modeling, and GenImage-scale lightweight benchmarks.
Our study isolates architecture effects within a shared low-data, no-pretraining training setting and reports generator-macro OOD, content-domain decomposition, worst-generator failure, JPEG~Q70 behavior, and measured batch-1/batch-32 efficiency.
